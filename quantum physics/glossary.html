Glossary

Atom

The smallest unit of matter that still has the properties of an element. Made of protons, neutrons, and electrons.

Electron

A tiny particle with a negative charge that moves around the nucleus of an atom.

Photon

A particle of light. It has no mass but carries energy.

Superposition

The ability of a quantum particle to be in more than one state at the same time until it is measured.

Entanglement

A special link between two particles so that the state of one instantly affects the other, no matter how far apart they are.

Wave Particle Duality

The idea that particles like electrons and photons can act both like waves and like particles, depending on how they are observed.

Quantum State

The complete set of information about a quantum system, including its possible positions, energies, and other properties.

Probability

In quantum physics, the chance of finding a particle in a certain place or state when measured.

Quantum Computer

A type of computer that uses quantum bits (qubits) to process information in ways that classical computers cannot.

Uncertainty Principle

A rule discovered by Werner Heisenberg stating that you cannot know both the exact position and exact speed of a particle at the same time.
